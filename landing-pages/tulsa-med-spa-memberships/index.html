The problem

Joining shouldn't feel like a gamble.

Tulsa-area memberships run from about $35 to $225 a month, and no two are built the same. Star ratings can't tell you what you'll pay. The terms can, if you can find them.

  • Does my fee turn into credit, or is it just gone?

    Some plans bank your monthly fee toward treatments. Others trade it for perks and a discount. Same price, very different math.

  • What's the member price on Botox?

    Public menus here list roughly $10 to $13 a unit. A dollar or two off per unit matters, but only once it beats the fee.

  • How do I get out?

    Minimum terms, notice periods, and what happens to unused credits are usually on page two. We put them on page one.

How we stay honest

Paid placement never touches the numbers.

Clinics can pay for a featured profile. They can't pay to change a price, hide a term, or move up a ranking, because there isn't one.

  • Every figure has a source and a date. You see where a number came from and when we last checked it.
  • Featured means featured. Sponsored profiles are labeled, every time.
  • Your email stays with us. A clinic only hears from you if you ask for a consultation.
  • We compare prices, not medicine. Nothing here is medical advice or a clinical endorsement. Check credentials and talk to a licensed provider.
