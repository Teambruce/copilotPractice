## 3. Shortlist: five opportunities
Buyer for all: **owner or GM of an owner-led US service business with 5–50 staff, who signs the cheque.** Each line narrows it further.

### 1. Missed-Call Rescue (Goldmine #1)
- Buyer: owner of an HVAC / plumbing / electrical company, 5–30 techs.
- Outcome: every missed or after-hours call gets answered, qualified and booked onto the schedule.
- Pay today: AI receptionists run about $30–$800/month; answering services more. [R]

### 2. Estimate Rescue (Goldmine #2)
- Buyer: owner of a roofing / remodeling / HVAC replacement company doing $1M–$10M, running Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan.
- Outcome: every unsigned estimate gets timed, personal follow-up in the owner's voice, and the owner sees which ones are worth a call today. Target: close rate moves toward top-quartile.
- Thing made more valuable: the estimates already sitting in their field-service software.

### 3. EDGE Scan: self-serve AI workflow audit (Goldmine #2, your expertise)
- Buyer: owner of a 5–50 person service business who wants AI but "doesn't know where to start".
- Outcome: in 30 minutes, a ranked list of their top 5 automation opportunities with hours saved, rough ROI and what to do first.
- Thing made more valuable: your existing AI Workflow Audit, run at scale. Also licensable to bookkeepers, MSPs and other consultants as a lead-gen tool.

### 4. Cash Collector (Goldmine #1)
- Buyer: owner or office manager at a B2B service firm on QuickBooks (commercial cleaning, IT/MSP, staffing, engineering), 10–50 staff.
- Outcome: days-to-paid drops; overdue invoices get relationship-aware reminders and escalation without the owner chasing.
- Pay today: staff time, AR tools, factoring fees. [A — not yet sourced]

### 5. Owner's Brain (Goldmine #3)
- Buyer: owner of a 10–40 person service business that is hiring.
- Outcome: owner talks through or screen-records a task once; AI produces the SOP and a team assistant that answers "how do we do X here?" by text or Slack. New hires ramp without pulling the owner in.
- Pay today: SOP/training platforms such as Trainual (reported ~$249+/month plus implementation). [R]

## 4. Key evidence gathered (P.R.O.F.I.T. light pass)
- Missed calls: home-service firms miss ~14–34% of calls, 27% most cited (Invoca data via vendor summaries). [R, secondary] https://www.signpost.com/blog/how-much-revenue-do-home-services-businesses-lose-from-missed-calls · https://ainora.lt/blog/hvac-service-call-statistics-2026
- AI receptionist pricing: Smith.ai from ~$95/mo, Rosie ~$49/mo, Goodcall ~$59–79/mo, Beside $29.99/mo. Crowded market. [R] https://getvoip.com/blog/ai-receptionist-pricing/ · https://www.eesel.ai/blog/ai-phone-receptionist
- Estimates: ServiceTitan 2025 benchmark (as cited by a vendor) shows top-quartile contractors close 54% of estimates vs 31% bottom quartile; roughly half of quotes get no follow-up. [R, vendor-cited] https://onepath.ai/blog/unsold-estimate-follow-up · https://www.joist.com/blog/how-to-follow-up-on-an-estimate-with-email-templates/
- AI adoption: 41% of UK small firms want AI but don't know where to start (Indeed); US small-business AI use fell from 42% to 28% (NEXT, 2025), citing cost and complexity. [R] https://www.digit.fyi/small-businesses-dont-know-where-to-start-with-ai-adoption/ · https://www.wftv.com/news/small-business-ai-adoption-declines-just-28/EPFURRPZJJKSJA24746DGKEFOM/
- Late payments: 56% of US small businesses are owed money, averaging $17.5K; 47% have invoices 30+ days late (QuickBooks 2025, n=2,487). [R] https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-late-payments-report-2025/
- Admin load: credible range 8–16 hrs/week of owner admin time. [R] https://www.business.com/articles/productivity-crisis-facing-small-business-leaders/
- Onboarding/SOPs: owner time per hire modeled at ~$1–2K; Trainual tiers reported $249–$399/mo (now quote-only). [R, vendor sources] https://www.whatstheprocessfor.com/blog/employee-onboarding-cost · https://costbench.com/software/learning-management/trainual/
